## Fatma Ezzahra Jlali

I work on languages and machines for places the tools skip. Lycée Pilote Manouba, Tunisia.

**Tunisian Arabic (Derja) NLP**

- [tunisianmmlu-audit](https://github.com/jjlalli/tunisianmmlu-audit): *Is TunisianMMLU Actually Tunisian? A Native-Speaker Audit of a Machine-Translated Dialect Benchmark*, with Marek Šuppa (MRL 2026, EMNLP). Two native speakers audited a 400-item sample of the only MMLU-style Derja benchmark; 62% to 86% of it is defective, mostly because the text is Moroccan, not Tunisian. Labels, corrected subset, scanner and evaluation code; data as [TunisianMMLU-Verified](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fatmajlali/TunisianMMLU-Verified).
- [Tunisian-Derja-NLP-Resources](https://github.com/jjlalli/Tunisian-Derja-NLP-Resources): an inventory of Derja NLP resources, 151 entries, each checked against its source and tagged for access. [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21779464) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fatmajlali/tunisian-nlp-resources). Contributions welcome.

**Water**

- [Saqi v2](https://github.com/jjlalli/Saqi-v2): offline irrigation controller for smallholder farms. One box at the manifold keeps a water account per soil zone and runs the pump itself. First field test on our olive grove: 29% less water than a timer. [Saqi v1](https://github.com/jjlalli/Saqi) is the simulation it grew out of (Moonshot 2026, Top 100).
- [Anzar](https://github.com/jjlalli/Anzar): battery-powered STM32 pipe sensor that tells a leak from normal use and catches taps left running, with no internet. Prototype units run at home and on the grove.
